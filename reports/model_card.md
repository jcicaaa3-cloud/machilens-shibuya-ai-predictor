# Model Card — MachiLens AI

MachiLens AI is a portfolio-oriented urban scenario forecasting prototype for Shibuya strategy comparison.

## Intended use

- Demonstrate an end-to-end AI workflow for urban analytics.
- Compare assumptions across A/B/C strategy scenarios.
- Provide a browser-friendly explanation for portfolio reviewers.

## Not intended use

- Official forecast
- Policy recommendation
- Investment or real-estate decision support
- Administrative or legal guidance
- Deterministic prediction of Shibuya's future

## Architecture

- Synthetic urban time-series generator
- Scenario and policy context vector
- Bidirectional LSTM encoder
- Temporal attention layer
- Fusion MLP
- Multi-task heads for success probability, scenario classification, flow change, and vacancy change

## Data

The public package uses synthetic data generated by code. It does not include copied maps, third-party report tables, private mobility/card datasets, or raw official datasets.

## Limitations

The current output depends on synthetic assumptions. Real deployment would require validated datasets, geospatial resolution, expert review, and careful monitoring.
