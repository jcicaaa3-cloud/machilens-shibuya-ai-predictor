# Limitations

MachiLens AI is a reference-only portfolio prototype.

- The sample data is synthetic.
- Outputs are scenario-comparison examples, not verified forecasts.
- The current version does not model block-level geospatial variation.
- Policy, tourism, culture, and real-estate dynamics are simplified.
- Results should not be used for actual policy, investment, administrative, or real-estate decisions.

A stronger future version would connect verified open data, spatial features, field validation, and expert review.
